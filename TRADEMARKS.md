# OnePlace name and logo

The OnePlace **code** is free software under GPL-3.0 with additional terms (see [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE](NOTICE)).
The **names** "OnePlace" and "buildcool", and the OnePlace **logo** and **app icon**, are not covered by that license:
they tell people that an app is the official one, made by buildcool.

## You may

- Say truthfully that your project is "based on OnePlace by buildcool" or "a fork of OnePlace".
- Link to https://github.com/buildcool/oneplace.
- Write about OnePlace, review it, and show screenshots of the official app.

## You may not (without written permission from buildcool)

- Publish an app, website or service named OnePlace, or with a name that can be mistaken for it.
- Use the OnePlace logo or icon, or the application id `io.github.buildcool.oneplace`, in your own builds.
- Say or suggest that your build is official, or made, published or endorsed by buildcool.
- Remove the "Made by buildcool" credit from the app.

## If you fork OnePlace

1. Change the app name, the application id (`android/app/build.gradle`, `capacitor.config.ts`) and the icon.
2. Keep [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE](NOTICE), and keep "Made by buildcool" in the About screen, saying that yours is modified (for example "Based on OnePlace by buildcool").
3. Publish your source code under the same license, as the GPL requires.

## Official builds

Only from https://github.com/buildcool/oneplace/releases (or the in-app updater, which downloads from there).
They are signed with buildcool's release certificate:

```
SHA-256 FB:4F:9C:9F:AA:5A:CE:29:48:21:28:FD:41:59:66:C2:8A:45:5E:99:3F:95:99:12:81:CE:D5:B2:07:F5:64:34
```

The app's About screen shows **Official build** only for these; any other copy shows **Unofficial copy**.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue at https://github.com/buildcool/oneplace/issues.
