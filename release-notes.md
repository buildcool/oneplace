## What's new

- **Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar, ZEE5, Spotify… links:** OnePlace now says right away that these are DRM-locked and can't be downloaded by any app (before, Netflix showed a confusing "network problem"). For songs it suggests YouTube Music or JioSaavn instead.
- The **Log in** button no longer shows for sites OnePlace has no login for (it did nothing).

### Also in this update (v1.0.5)

- **Playlists save as they go:** every finished video/song appears in Movies/Music right away instead of at the end.
- **A few broken videos no longer lose the whole playlist:** deleted or private items are skipped, the rest are kept, and the download says "some items failed". **Retry** then fetches only the missing items (no duplicates).
- Paused or interrupted playlists resume where they stopped.
- Photo/profile downloads (gallery-dl) also save each file as soon as it finishes.
