## What's new

- **Copy a link → it downloads.** Copy a video link anywhere and open OnePlace: the download starts by itself with your usual settings, no taps. (Since Android 10 no app may look at what you copy while it is in the background, so opening OnePlace is the earliest it can start.) Switch it off in More → Downloads.
- **"Download copied link" tile:** copy a link in any app, pull down Quick Settings and tap the tile. It downloads without leaving the app you are in. Add it from More → Share.
- **YouTube links read about twice as fast:** ~3.5–4 s instead of ~6–8 s, and no more ~14 s first read when YouTube changes its player. Same qualities up to 4K, same audio, same song tags. If YouTube ever refuses, the old way is used automatically.

### From v1.0.8 (if you skipped it)

- Downloads reuse what the sheet already read (first bytes ~1.5 s after tapping Download), **Start now** buttons while a link is being read, faster MP3 covers, and every site starts ~2 s faster.
